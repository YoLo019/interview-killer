# Question Template

Use this exact structure for every question card:

```md
### Q{n}. {question title}

**Question**  
{full interview question}

**What It Tests**  
{what the interviewer is checking}

**One-Minute Skeleton**  
- {point 1}
- {point 2}
- {point 3}

**Expanded Answer**  
{spoken, interview-ready answer}

**Possible Follow-Ups**  
- {follow-up 1}
- {follow-up 2}

**Follow-Up Quick Answers**  
- {follow-up 1}: {interview-usable short answer, usually a small paragraph the candidate can speak for up to about 2 minutes}
- {follow-up 2}: {interview-usable short answer, usually a small paragraph the candidate can speak for up to about 2 minutes}

**Why This Is Likely**  
{why this question is plausible for this candidate}
```

Rules:

- Use spoken language, not essay style.
- Keep the skeleton compact enough to memorize.
- Keep the expanded answer specific enough to sound grounded.
- Keep each quick follow-up answer substantial enough to stand on its own as a small spoken paragraph, but still compact enough to be spoken in under about 2 minutes.
- If support is weak, say what is uncertain.
